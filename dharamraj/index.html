Our Works

Environmental Factors

Dharamraj Foundation promotes a cleaner, greener future through Swachh Bharat, Water Conservation, Environment Protection, Sanitation & Community Development, along with eco-friendly initiatives like GoGreen products and Solar Power Systems.

Human Well-Being Efforts

Dharmaraj Foundation works for human well-being through disaster relief, digital education & women empowerment, free legal support, and promoting arts, culture & animal welfare—uplifting lives, protecting rights, and nurturing compassion in society.
