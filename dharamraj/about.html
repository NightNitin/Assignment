About Us

Dharmaraj Foundation collaborates with a diverse array of Partners such as NGOs, Government and Corporate CSR Programs to drive impactful community development initiatives across India. These partnerships focus on areas such as education, healthcare, women's empowerment, and environmental sustainability.
